# Risks

PONS launch tokens are user-created and experimental.

## Before Launching

- Verify Robinhood Chain and the transaction destination.
- Confirm the creator fee wallet.
- Review any initial creator buy and resulting concentration.
- Confirm the live fee, pool settings, and graduation threshold.
- Record the deployed token and pool addresses.

## Before Trading

- Verify the token contract rather than the name or ticker.
- Review creator and holder concentration.
- Confirm pool liquidity.
- Review the quoted amount, price impact, and slippage.
- Understand that received amounts can differ from quotes.
- Do not assume graduation implies safety or future demand.

## Irreversible Actions

Blockchain transactions may be irreversible. Never share a private key, seed phrase, password, or authentication token. PONS documentation states that it will not ask users to send funds to process a community takeover application.

## No Guarantees

Locked liquidity, buy protection, fee rewards, protocol buybacks, burns, and graduation do not guarantee token quality, price appreciation, profitability, or an exit.
